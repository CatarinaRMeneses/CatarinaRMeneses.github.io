I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at [Northeastern University](https://www.northeastern.edu/), in the [Department of Economics](https://cssh.northeastern.edu/economics/). 

My primary research interests are in crime, health and gender economics. 

I study how institutional responses to violence shape victim and perpetrator outcomes. My research spans public health and criminal justice, examining questions such as how access to substance use treatment affects intimate partner violence, how quickly police respond to domestic violence calls, and how the speed of an arrest after a violent crime shapes future crime in the surrounding area. I use administrative data and quasi-experimental methods to identify causal effects.
